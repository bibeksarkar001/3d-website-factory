# Tallybot (demo)

## What it is
Tallybot is a made-up brand for testing this folder. It is fictional and the site must say so in the footer.

Tallybot is a small desk robot for shop owners. You tell it a sale out loud ("two teas, one samosa"),
it writes the bill, adds it to the day's total and shows the running total on its face.
At closing time it reads the day back to you.

For: owners of small shops and stalls who do not want to type on a phone with busy hands.

No prices, customer names, numbers or reviews. Do not add any.
