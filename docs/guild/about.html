How Guild is run.

Six house rules, written down. They are the whole constitution.

Help goes round. You get two reviews, you give two, and the ring keeps turning.

  1. §1

    Thursdays are for building.

    Build nights run online at 19:00 in New York, London and Singapore. Come with something to work on.

  2. §2

    Two reviews in, two out.

    Every project gets written feedback from two members within a week. You review two in return.

  3. §3

    Say where you're stuck.

    Post in #stuck with what you tried. Short questions get fast answers.

  4. §4

    Nobody is selling.

    No sponsored posts and no vendor channels. Recommend a tool only if you used it.

  5. §5

    People read applications.

    A member reads each one and answers within a week.

  6. §6

    Ship small, show it.

    Unfinished is fine. Post what works today; the reviewers will help with tomorrow.
