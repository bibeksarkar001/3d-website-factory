Six layers, one job each.

  1. Knit upperOne piece of engineered knit. Stretch where the foot spreads, firm at the heel.
  2. Lacing cageFive straps a side tie the laces to the sole, so the knit can stay soft.
  3. Carbon plateFull length and curved. It stiffens the forefoot and tips you onto the toe.
  4. Foam midsoleNitrogen-infused foam: most of the stack, light and quick to spring back.
  5. Cushion podA firmer layer under the soft one, so the shoe stays steady when you land.
  6. OutsoleThin rubber with a shallow lug, thicker where heels wear first.

Is a carbon plate only for racing?

No. Stride is built as a daily trainer for long and steady runs that also works on race day. The plate is curved and slightly softer than a racing flat's, so easy kilometres feel fine.

How does it fit?

True to size, with a roomy toe box. If you are between sizes, go up half a size for marathon distances; feet swell late in a long run.

How long does the foam last?

Plan on roughly 500 to 700 km, like most high-stack trainers. The firmer pod underneath helps the soft foam keep its shape for longer.

Can I run on trails?

Light gravel and park paths, yes. The shallow lug is made for road; for mud or rock you will want a trail shoe.
