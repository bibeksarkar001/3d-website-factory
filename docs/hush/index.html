How long does the battery last?

Up to thirty hours of listening with noise cancelling on.

How does noise cancelling work?

Six microphones hear the noise around you and the headphones play its opposite, so the two cancel. See it drawn.

Can I replace the ear cushions?

Yes. The memory-foam cushions come off and new ones go on.
