Is it ready to use?

Yes. It arrives seasoned with three baked-on coats of oil.

Will it work on induction?

Yes, and on gas, electric, in the oven and over a fire.

Why is the inside smooth?

The cooking floor is machined after casting, so it is flat and smooth.

How heavy is it?

2.4 kg. The weight is what holds the heat.
