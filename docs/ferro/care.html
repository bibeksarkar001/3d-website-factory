Wipe. Oil. Warm.

That is all it asks. Do it after cooking and it gets better every time.

  1. Wipe

    While it is still warm, wipe it out. Hot water if it needs it.

  2. Oil

    A few drops of oil, rubbed thin over the cooking floor.

  3. Warm

    A minute on low heat so the oil settles in. Then it is ready for next time.
