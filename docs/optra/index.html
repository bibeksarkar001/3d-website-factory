Questions.

Does it autofocus?

No. The Optra 35 is manual focus only, with an all-metal ring.

How close can it focus?

To 0.3 m.

Why eleven blades?

More blades make a rounder opening when you stop down, so out-of-focus lights stay round.

Which cameras?

It is made for mirrorless cameras. Ask us about your mount when you sign up.

What filters fit?

Any 52 mm screw-in filter.
