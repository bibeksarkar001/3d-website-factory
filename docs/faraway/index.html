Good questions.

How big are the groups?

Ten travellers at most, with one local host.

How do we get there?

By the island's scheduled ferry, the same boat the island uses.

Where do we stay?

In family-run guesthouses, and you eat where the island eats.

How much walking is there?

You walk most days. The longest is the headland walk out to the lighthouse.

When do trips run?

From April to October, for seven or ten nights.
