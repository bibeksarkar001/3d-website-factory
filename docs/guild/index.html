Find your Thursday.

Pick the build night nearest you. It runs at 19:00 local time, every week.

Before you apply.

Do I need to be a programmer?

No. Members range from people on their first project to people who write code for a living. What matters is that you are building something.

What happens at a build night?

An online room opens at 19:00 local time. You say what you are working on, then you work. When you get stuck, you say so out loud and someone helps.

Who reads my application?

A member does, not an algorithm. You hear back within a week.

What do I owe in return?

Two reviews for every project you post. That is how everyone gets two.

Will anyone try to sell me a tool?

No. There are no sponsored posts and no vendor channels. It is in the house rules, section 4.

Bring the thing you're stuck on.

A short application. A member reads it and answers within a week.
