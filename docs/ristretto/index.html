Before you come in.

Can I sit down?

No. Ristretto is a standing bar. There is room for about eight people along it.

Do you make long drinks?

No. The longest drink on the board is a cortado. Every drink starts with the same short shot.

What coffee are you pouring?

One coffee a month. It is written on the board, and in the monthly note.

Can I book?

Walk in. Groups of six or more can ask ahead on the visit page.

When are you open?

7 to 15, Tuesday to Sunday.
