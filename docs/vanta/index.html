Before you drive it.

How far does it go on a charge?

Up to 480 km on the WLTP cycle, from a 92 kWh battery that sits under the floor.

How quickly does it charge?

The GT runs on an 800-volt system. On a fast charger it goes from 10 to 80 % in 18 minutes.

How many people fit?

Two doors and 2+2 seats: two up front, two smaller seats behind.

Which colours and wheels can I choose?

Four paints: Copper Flare, Arctic, Graphite and Signal Green. The 21-inch ten-spoke wheels come in satin black or polished.

Is Vanta a real car?

No. Vanta is a fictional brand, made to demo 3D Website Factory. Every 3D object on this page is built in code.
