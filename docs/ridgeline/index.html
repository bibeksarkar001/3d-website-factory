Before you ride.

How fast does the motor help?

It assists up to 25 km/h. Above that, it is just you.

Can I charge the battery off the bike?

Yes. The 720 Wh battery slides out from below the down tube, so you can carry it in and charge it anywhere.

Why two wheel sizes?

A 29-inch front rolls over roots and rocks; a 27.5-inch rear keeps the back end quick through turns.

How much does it weigh?

23.4 kg in size M.

Which colours and sizes?

Pine, Granite or Rust, in S, M, L and XL.
