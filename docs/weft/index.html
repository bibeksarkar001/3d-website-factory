How a pair gets made.

  1. Woven narrow

    The cloth comes off narrow shuttle looms, so both edges close themselves with a clean selvedge and an ochre line.

  2. Cut as one leg

    One straight leg, cut with the selvedge on the outseam, where it shows when you roll the cuff.

  3. Sewn in ochre

    Cotton thread at about nine stitches to the inch, in twin rows where the seams work hardest.

  4. Riveted

    Copper rivets at the pocket corners.

  5. Hemmed to you

    A chain-stitched hem, and every pair comes with free hemming.

Before you order.

What does raw mean?

The cloth has not been washed. It is stiffer and darker at first, and it fades where you bend, sit and carry things, so the pair ends up yours.

Raw or once-rinsed?

Raw if you want the fades to be all your own. Once-rinsed if you would rather start a little softer.

Which sizes?

Waist 28 to 38, in one straight-leg fit.

Do you hem them?

Yes. Every pair comes with free hemming, with a chain-stitched hem.

Why small runs?

The cloth is woven on narrow shuttle looms and each run is sewn in one go. Join the list and we will tell you when the next one opens.
