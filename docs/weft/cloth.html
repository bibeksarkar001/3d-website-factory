The cloth.

A heavy right-hand twill, woven narrow on shuttle looms. Turn it over in your hand.

14.5 oz

Heavy enough to hold a crease and a shape, and to fade where you bend.

3 over 1

Each indigo warp thread floats over three weft threads and under one.

Raw

Unwashed. Stiff and dark at first, then it moves to the way you wear it.

An edge that closes itself.

A shuttle loom passes one continuous weft thread back and forth, so the cloth finishes its own edges. That edge is the selvedge, and ours carries an ochre line.
