How we travel.

Slowly. In a small group, on the island's own boats, at the island's pace.

  1. Ten, at most

    Every group is ten travellers at most, so the island barely notices you arrive.

  2. One local host

    Each group travels with one host from the island.

  3. The scheduled ferry

    You arrive the way everyone else does, on the regular boat.

  4. Family-run rooms

    You stay in family-run guesthouses and eat where the island eats.

  5. On foot

    You walk most days, and one day goes all the way out to the lighthouse.
